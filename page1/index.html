An MCC (Motor Control Center) panel is an electrical panel used to control, protect, and operate electric motors in industrial facilities. It includes circuit breakers, contactors, overload relays, and control devices to ensure safe and efficient motor operation.

SPWM (Sinusoidal Pulse Width Modulation) is a technique used to control inverter output by comparing a sinusoidal reference signal with a high-frequency carrier wave. It produces an AC output with controllable voltage and frequency, widely used in motor drives and power electronics.

A JK flip-flop is a type of sequential logic circuit used for data storage and state control in digital systems. It can toggle its output when both J and K inputs are high, making it useful in counters and timing applications.
